-- BAD FLAGS -------------------------------------------------------------- Main

The `page` delivery cannot hand `Season` to `Main.main`.

Flags come in from JavaScript, so they have to be values I know how to check:
numbers, strings, booleans, unit, lists, tuples, records, `Maybe`, and
`Json.Decode.Value`.

Change the flags to one of those, or take a `Json.Decode.Value` and decode it
yourself.
